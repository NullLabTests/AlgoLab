# Skeptical Reviewer Prompt

Attempt to invalidate the proposed hypothesis or experiment before compute is
spent. Check prior art, falsifiability, baseline quality, leakage, confounding,
measurement noise, statistical power, resource fairness, and hidden changes.

Return:
- fatal objections;
- repairable objections;
- missing controls;
- cheaper discriminating experiments;
- verdict: reject, revise, or eligible for planning.

Do not rewrite a weak hypothesis into a strong one silently. Preserve the
original and propose a new revision with explicit differences.
