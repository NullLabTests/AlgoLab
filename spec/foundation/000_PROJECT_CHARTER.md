# Project Charter

AlgoLab exists to turn AI-assisted ideation into reliable experimental science.
It prioritizes evidence over volume, bounded autonomy over uncontrolled loops,
and reusable negative knowledge over forgotten failure.

## Deliverable
A working, auditable laboratory that can discover or rediscover algorithmic
improvements on small-scale AI systems.

## Out of scope for v1
- training frontier foundation models;
- autonomous spending outside hard caps;
- unrestricted internet or shell access;
- claims of AGI or recursive intelligence explosion;
- publication without human review;
- replacing scientific judgment with a single model response.

## Stakeholders
- Operator: owns budget and final approval.
- Research users: propose objectives and inspect evidence.
- Implementers: maintain platform code.
- Reviewers: audit methods and claims.
- External researchers: reproduce published artifacts.

## Completion definition
The project is complete only when a clean checkout can reproduce the reference
rediscovery experiment and its report using documented commands.
